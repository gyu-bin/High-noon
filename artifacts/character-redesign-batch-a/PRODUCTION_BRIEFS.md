## P04 — 망령 사수 / Phantom Sharpshooter

### Current failure / current silhouette

Opaque black longcoat, red scarf, familiar hat and symmetric boots. P01's longcoat
construction with darker clothes. Nothing in the shape communicates a returning
ghost; the revive ability is not an excuse for ordinary-looking art.

### Proposed identity lock — THREE primary anchors only

1. Two separated torn **floating longcoat tails**, detached from normal gravity.
2. Hollow, lightless face beneath the familiar hat; no ordinary visible skin.
3. Partially spectral lower legs with interrupted outer contours, not uniform
   transparency applied to the entire cowboy.

### New target silhouette

A recognizable upright Western gunman whose long coat opens into two hanging,
lifted tail ribbons with clear empty gaps. Belt/torso form the stable centre.
The coat hem and lower-leg contours break ordinary weight-bearing geometry.
Both upper shoulders remain coherent: do not copy Shadow Hunter's missing side.

### Production brief

- Identity lock: same Western hat, red neck-cloth remnant, longcoat lineage,
  leather belt and single holster. The redesigned ghost structure is mandatory;
  simply tracing the current P04 and adding rim light is a rejection.
- Palette: charcoal/black with pale ash and restrained blue-white **material**
  transitions at spectral boundaries. Small weathered brown leather and dull brass
  preserve HIGH NOON roots. Red scarf becomes a muted residual accent, not a glow.
- Clothing: recognizable longcoat, visible vest/waist, two lifted torn tails.
  No poncho, armor plates, skull costume or giant shroud.
- Face/head: familiar hat silhouette; an empty shadow face with no human skin.
  No extra eyes: red glowing eyes are intentionally NOT another selected anchor.
- Weapon: classic revolver in worn leather holster, cold worn-steel barrel and
  weathered wood grip. No separate ghost gun effect in this first identity brief.
- Supernatural: only the three anchors above. No broad smoke cloud/aura obscuring
  the body. Cold edge is narrow material breakup, not a neon contour.
- Pose: source-like IDLE quick-draw readiness; arms relaxed, neither firing nor
  falling. Slight unnatural coat motion, not a levitating superhero pose.
- Transparency: background fully clear; lower-leg material may be selectively
  translucent; preserve readable boot remnants and coherent belt/torso/head.
- Resolution: native square ≥1254×1254 true-alpha PNG, no aggressive downsample.
